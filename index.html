GRAIN is a creative R&D laboratory.

We build creative solutions to frontier technology:
production, implementation, consultation and collaboration.

AI systems represent an increasingly viable tool option for creative video and image generation pipelines. Knowing how to work with them requires a combination of frontier technical expertise and deep creative experience.

What We Do

We bring multidisciplinary backgrounds in creative technology, filmmaking and photography, and years of process-oriented experience, to the open territory of emerging AI tools.

When there is an obvious solution, we implement it. More often we work on problems without one, and find the creative path that gets there.

  • Production. Finished films and imagery, from look development to final delivery.
  • Implementation. Custom pipelines and workflows built around the team and the job.
  • Consultation. A clear read on what is possible now and what is worth pursuing.
  • Collaboration. Ongoing partnerships with artists, studios, brands and research labs.

How We Work

  • Problem first. We start from the creative challenge, not from a tool.
  • Cross-platform. We work across models, research and platforms, so every pipeline fits the job.
  • Any scale. From a single consultation to full production, working alongside existing craft departments.

Partners & Collaborators
